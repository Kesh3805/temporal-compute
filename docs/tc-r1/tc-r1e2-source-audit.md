# E2 source audit before results

Audit applies only to pinned upstream `b4ce9687e6c695f5582997c61b0c66cf064bdb4a` and the envelope in `tc-r1e2-contract.md`. It does not certify arbitrary PBRT scenes.

Native CPU `RayIntegrator::EvaluatePixelSample` generates a camera ray, calls native `Li`, and sends its weighted spectral value through RGB Film.AddSample. RGB Film converts to sensor RGB, accumulates weighted sums/weight, and converts the normalized sensor mean to output RGB. With unclamped sensor values, no splats and unit box weights, that conversion is linear: the count-weighted mean of batch Film RGBs represents the same estimator as the combined sample set. E2 independently compares native Film outputs to means of Film.ToOutputRGB samples; numerical tolerance is frozen in the earlier pre-test commit. Sampler configuration remains fixed so ray-differential scaling does not change by batch.

Native `PathIntegrator::Li` has one regular intersection at each loop entry and one continuation SpawnRay after BSDF sampling. The continuation is constructed before the Russian-roulette test: it is charged even when roulette stops the loop. Native `SampleLd` samples a light and invokes Unoccluded only after nonzero valid radiance/BSDF/PDF. `Integrator::Unoccluded` is exactly `!IntersectP(p0.SpawnRayTo(p1), 1 - ShadowEpsilon)`: one visibility generation per query. The E2 visibility hook counts that operation. MIS/PDF calculations and BSDF direction sampling do not trace additional rays in this envelope.

Excluded paths: null BSDF SkipIntersection; media transmittance Tr; BSSRDF probe segments; visible-surface albedo auxiliary sampling; bidirectional/photon specialized integrators. RGB Film does not request VisibleSurface, disabling its auxiliary albedo sample path. Diffuse/conductor/dielectric surface BSDF sampling does not itself cast scene rays. Geometry traversal may test many primitives per traced ray; those tests are not separate constructed rendering rays and are not counted as such.

The common generated-ray count is incremented by E2Charge at camera, continuation and visibility operation sites. Independently retained class events and native per-sample Intersect/IntersectP counter deltas test coverage and arithmetic. These are direct call-site deltas, not a parsing of final renderer statistics or an spp model. All generated continuations remain charged; traced continuations cannot exceed them. Empty-fixture analytic counts and required continuation/visibility/roulette fixtures guard against merely agreeing redundant counters. No allocation policy/probes exist in this gate; future probes must enter the same operation sites.

Limits: this audit must be combined with runtime fixture evidence, not substituted for it. The current tests do not explicitly identify reflection versus transmission flags or exercise triangle meshes; those coverage omissions must be disclosed and prevent claiming proof for the complete envelope solely from a green fixture summary. A fixture failure or missing complete coverage stops this bounded gate; do not rewrite the renderer or relax the envelope after results.
