## Accounting contract and limits

Charge every active traced ray segment: first camera intersection, continuation intersections and visibility/shadow tests, including allocation/probe work. For this pinhole, opaque, no-media path scope, total = regular intersection calls + visibility calls; camera is a subset of regular and continuation = regular − camera. Do not add camera twice. These are whole ray queries, not BVH nodes or primitive intersection tests. Probe count is zero because these probes run no allocation policy; future probe-generated rays must enter the same counters. Counter deltas are thread-local and recorded around each native sample; logging is mutex-protected. No counters are inferred from spp.

The empty fixture has 512 camera/regular rays and zero continuation/visibility. Diffuse and specular central crops each have 512 camera and 512 continuation rays. The point-light crop adds 246 visibility rays. Full-render native zero counters are omitted by PBRT's statistics printer; the parser correction treats omitted entries as zero only when the statistics section exists. Per-sample image-space coordinates/sample indices are retained and checked for duplicates/outside-region work.

The stock Film still receives samples. Our diagnostic logging uses Film.ToOutputRGB, but does not prove correct progressive accumulation, adaptive stopping, differential scaling, clamping/filter equivalence or merged image normalization. Nonzero-begin output images are expressly invalid for production use. This prevents selecting PBRT now despite promising stream/counter mechanics. The next task is a persistent common accumulation adapter with independent uniform/Film parity and extended ray-accounting tests, not a controller comparison.

## Measurements versus projections

PBRT measures three complete 64×64×256-spp runs per unrelated fixture, including process startup/render/output in wall time. Median camera throughput ranges from approximately 1.39 million/s (sphere fixtures) to 2.07 million/s (empty). Initial 60-reference-frame cost extrapolates to approximately 4.3–6.5 hours on those fixtures, **not measured corpus cost**. The build log reports memory; per-render utilization/RSS and analysis/storage scaling are not fully measured. Full sample logging is disabled during throughput runs, so this is native-kernel throughput, not an instrumented controller end-to-end timing claim.

Expanded Mitsuba probes use 64×64×4096-spp workloads, one cold render and three warm renders per synthetic empty/diffuse/specular/point-light fixture. Linux scalar measures about 9.0–24.0 million camera samples/s; LLVM about 36.6–218.7 million/s. JIT timing calls dr.eval and synchronizes before start/after completion with dr.sync_thread; cold initialization/load are separate. These renderers use different spectral/RGB kernels, thread behavior and host environments, so raw rates are not a fair policy or kernel-quality comparison. Rays/s remains unavailable for Mitsuba. No GPU figures are inferred.

Reference projection is 32,212,254,720 / measured camera samples/s, before convergence escalation to 16K/32K spp. A 256×256×8192-spp frame is 536,870,912 camera samples; its two-stream cost is twice that quotient. Production projection must instead use measured actual rays/s: 240 frame renders per policy × maximum ray checkpoint 2^26 (checkpoints are cumulative, do not sum restarted runs unless the final runner actually restarts). Multiply by all registered policies/ablations and timing repetitions. Since representative ray/production overhead feasibility is not yet established, no complete experiment completion-time promise is made.

## Remaining work and decision

Neither current adapter qualifies the first four validity requirements for execution. Stop before renderer selection; preserve both candidates and requirements. PBRT is the more transparent next instrumentation candidate, but is **not selected**. Mitsuba's measured LLVM speed is useful feasibility evidence, but cannot waive complete charged-ray accounting.

Still required: persistent common sample accumulation/Film parity; audited native ray counters for every admitted feature/backend; robust paired stream definitions; identical observation adapters and minimum-coverage/ranking/additional-work mechanics; estimator/stopping validation; measured representative hardware/memory/storage/trace/analysis costs. Then choose a renderer/backend with validity first. Final assets/hashes/licenses, final controller and strong baselines/ablations, converged independent references, metric/model hashes, hardware/timing freeze, censoring analysis and complete retained-output runner remain outside this qualification PR. No execution tag is created. Issue #9 stays open.
