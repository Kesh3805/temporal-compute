# TC-S1 evidence dossier

Retrieved 2026-10-06. The corpus is independently specified **from scheduling policy**, not sampled from a mission's operational telemetry. External grounding establishes credible product classes and motivations; it does not turn our generated jobs into measured spacecraft workloads.

Provenance: **A** directly reported mission observation/measurement; **B** inference from sourced mission facts; **C** externally supported engineering range/prototype result; **D** experimental assumption; **E** synthetic control. For qualitative application existence, A means an authoritative mission statement, not a runtime measurement.

No measured per-task mission runtime, arrival process, utility weight, critical-success threshold or freshness lifetime is imported into the generator. Every numerical corpus parameter is D; all control values are E. A/B support class existence and qualitative motivation only. There are no C-derived numerical distributions in the primary corpus. Public evidence does not establish that useful EO products expire on our chosen seconds-scale; this is explicitly a stress-model assumption tested by sensitivity analysis.
